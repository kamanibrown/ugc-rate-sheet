03 / Terms

Good to know. How the money works.

The terms that sit behind every figure on this sheet, stated up front so the quote you see is the quote you pay.

All figures in USD

Every price on this sheet — the base rate, the packages and every add-on — is quoted in US dollars.

Quotes are net to Kamani

The quoted figure is what Kamani receives. Payment-processing fees are on the brand or grossed up in the quote — never deducted from the rate.

Payment by bank transfer

Payment by bank transfer (Wise) is preferred, and PayPal and Payoneer are also available. Full payment details are shared privately once a booking is confirmed.

Organic vs. paid use

The base rate covers organic use on the brand’s own channels and product pages. Any paid advertising use — including whitelisted or boosted posts — requires the usage rights add-on.
